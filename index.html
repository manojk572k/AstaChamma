🎲 Ashtachamma · Rule Based Game

Classic Indian board game with modern twist. Features:

  • Select number of players (1-4) and who starts
  • Dice: 1,2,3,4,8 – start only on 4/8
  • Extra turn on 4/8 or capture
  • Exact finish at centre cell (c3)
  • Penalty after three consecutive 4/8
  • Safe cells: a3, c1, c5, e3 (marked X)
  • Three colour themes – use switcher above
