📖 Ashtachamma Rules

  • Choose 1 to 4 active players and set the starting player.
  • Only rolls of 4 or 8 can bring a token out of home.
  • Landing on an enemy token captures it unless the cell is safe.
  • Safe cells are marked with X.
  • You must land exactly on the center finish cell.
  • Three consecutive 4/8 rolls cause a penalty.
